# Brand Facts: the 12 questions

Adapted from the Factor8 facts-form. Use selected questions during the optional brand workshop, not as a required interview. Skip what is unknown or irrelevant; confirm useful sourced drafts with the user.

## Identity and offer

1. **brand_name**: What is your brand name?
2. **type_of_business**: What is the type (or main focus) of your business? Help: what is the primary solution or service your business offers? Example: "Outsourced Growth Team, Website Building Framework for Modular HubSpot Websites, Cybersecurity Software."
3. **target_market**: Describe the market that you serve. The specific group of people you want to sell your solution to. Be specific for best results. Example: "Founders and CMOs of SaaS companies in the US or Canada, using or willing to use HubSpot."
4. **competitors**: Who are the primary direct competitors customers are likely to consider? Names or URLs are useful. Include only competitors the user knows or sources support; no minimum count.

## Level 2

5. **pain_points**: List the top 2-5 challenges or problems your company helps customers overcome. Pain points and problems, not goals or desires. Example: "Weak buyer messaging; underperforming websites; poor lead quality; lack of lead volume. Bottom line: their marketing is unprofitable."
6. **underlying_issues**: What are the fundamental reasons behind why your target buyers have these pain points? Help: identify the root causes that lead to the problems your solution solves.
7. **competitor_promise_failures**: What specific gaps or shortcomings do your competitors' solutions have? Help: under 50 words, be specific about where they underperform. Example: "Focus on vanity metrics at the expense of revenue; high costs with unclear ROI; charging human rates while humans use ChatGPT."
8. **supporting_data**: Do you have data or stats which demonstrate the need for a quality solution? Help: third-party stats that prove the problem is significant. NOTE for the drafting agent: never invent these; leave blank if the scrape has none.
9. **list_of_solutions**: List your solutions and briefly describe the main benefits of each one.

## Level 3

10. **business_outcomes**: How have those outcomes actually impacted your business?
11. **specific_results**: What tangible outcomes do customers experience after using your solution? Help: real improvements, real numbers where they exist. NOTE for the drafting agent: draft only from case studies and testimonials actually found; never fabricate numbers.
12. **training_or_webinar**: Do you have a video training or webinar that walks buyers through your solution? Title and outcomes.
