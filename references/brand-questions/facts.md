# Brand Facts: the 12 questions

Exported verbatim from the Factor8 facts-form. Levels: 1 = basic, 2 = intermediate, 3 = advanced. The onboard skill asks these one at a time on the company path, drafting from the site scrape first where it can.

## Level 1 (scrape can usually draft all of these)

1. **brand_name** (required): What is your brand name?
2. **type_of_business** (required): What is the type (or main focus) of your business? Help: what is the primary solution or service your business offers? Example: "Outsourced Growth Team, Website Building Framework for Modular HubSpot Websites, Cybersecurity Software."
3. **target_market** (required): Describe the market that you serve. The specific group of people you want to sell your solution to. Be specific for best results. Example: "Founders and CMOs of SaaS companies in the US or Canada, using or willing to use HubSpot."
4. **competitors** (required): Who are the primary direct competitors customers are likely to consider? Help: URLs only, aim for at least 5, put the lowest-cost provider last.

## Level 2

5. **pain_points** (required): List the top 2-5 challenges or problems your company helps customers overcome. Pain points and problems, not goals or desires. Example: "Weak buyer messaging; underperforming websites; poor lead quality; lack of lead volume. Bottom line: their marketing is unprofitable."
6. **underlying_issues** (required): What are the fundamental reasons behind why your target buyers have these pain points? Help: identify the root causes that lead to the problems your solution solves.
7. **competitor_promise_failures** (required): What specific gaps or shortcomings do your competitors' solutions have? Help: under 50 words, be specific about where they underperform. Example: "Focus on vanity metrics at the expense of revenue; high costs with unclear ROI; charging human rates while humans use ChatGPT."
8. **supporting_data** (required): Do you have data or stats which demonstrate the need for a quality solution? Help: third-party stats that prove the problem is significant. NOTE for the drafting agent: never invent these; leave blank if the scrape has none.
9. **list_of_solutions** (required): List your solutions and briefly describe the main benefits of each one.

## Level 3

10. **business_outcomes** (optional): How have those outcomes actually impacted your business?
11. **specific_results** (required): What tangible outcomes do customers experience after using your solution? Help: real improvements, real numbers where they exist. NOTE for the drafting agent: draft only from case studies and testimonials actually found; never fabricate numbers.
12. **training_or_webinar** (optional): Do you have a video training or webinar that walks buyers through your solution? Title and outcomes.
