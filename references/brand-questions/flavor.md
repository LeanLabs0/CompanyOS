# Brand Flavor: the voice interview

Two parts. Part A is the 30-question voice-archetype quiz (exported from the Factor8 flavors quiz; the DB stores the chosen answer per question, so the onboard presents each as an open A-or-B style choice and DRAFTS the likely answer from the site's copy tone). Part B elicits the enforcement lists.

## Part A: the 30 archetype questions

1. When explaining complex topics, we: (e.g. start with data, frameworks, and strategic analysis / tell stories first)
2. Our primary goal with content is to: (educate / challenge people to question their assumptions)
3. We prefer content that: (showcases deep knowledge and proven expertise / feels approachable and simple)
4. When clients have problems, we: (analyze and recommend our expert solution / coach them to their own answer)
5. Our natural communication style is: (friendly and conversational, like talking to a colleague / polished and formal)
6. When we see outdated industry practices, we: (scrap them completely and start from scratch / improve them incrementally)
7. Our competitive advantage comes from: (years of expertise and battle-tested strategies / fresh thinking)
8. We stand out in the market by: (building innovative solutions through creative teamwork / out-executing on fundamentals)
9. Our ideal client relationship feels like: (working alongside a trusted business partner / a vendor delivering to spec)
10. When we encounter industry-wide problems, we: (research root causes and provide strategic solutions / rally the community)
11. The core of what we offer is: (step-by-step education to help people succeed / done-for-you outcomes)
12. People choose us because: (we truly get their world and speak their language / we have the biggest name)
13. Our content style focuses on: (inspiring people to build and create new things / warning about risks)
14. When talking about change, we emphasize: (smart, strategic improvements over time / dramatic transformation)
15. We present ourselves as: (the trusted expert with proven answers / the fellow traveler figuring it out)
16. Our typical message to audiences is: ("what you're doing isn't working, here's why" / "you're closer than you think")
17. Our approach to industry standards is: (offer better alternatives to current best practices / defend the fundamentals)
18. When we launch something new, we highlight: (the expert strategy and proven methodology behind it / the shiny outcome)
19. In a crisis, we: (provide calm, clear instructions on what to do next / rally emotion and urgency)
20. When competitors criticize us publicly, we: (respond authentically and keep it professional / punch back)
21. At industry events, we're known for: (sharing valuable insights and proven strategies / entertainment value)
22. Our sales conversations typically focus on: (teaching prospects about their options and best practices / closing)
23. In our case studies, we emphasize: (how we fundamentally changed their approach / the headline number)
24. We want to be known as the company that: (provides the clearest strategic guidance in our industry / moves fastest)
25. Our ideal reputation would be: (the helpful guides who make success feel achievable / the elite specialists)
26. We'll know we've succeeded when: (we're the most trusted, go-to partner in our space / we're the biggest)
27. After experiencing our content, people should feel: (equipped with practical tools for immediate action / inspired)
28. We create the most value when we're: (inspiring bold action and breakthrough thinking / preventing mistakes)
29. Our long-term vision is to be: (the most trusted, relatable partner people turn to / the category king)
30. (Archetype read-out: the quiz scores toward an accent like "Guide". The onboard states the inferred archetype and confirms it.)

## Part B: the enforcement lists (humans only; the scrape can suggest, never decide)

31. **never_words** (required): Words and phrases your company never uses. Seeds the bad-copy list that CI and reviews enforce.
32. **always_words**: Words, phrases, or framings you always prefer. Example: "growth agency" not "full-service agency".
33. **voice_samples** (required): Paste 1-2 pieces of PUBLISHED copy the company considers perfectly on-voice. Published artifacts only, never text typed during this conversation (the anti-contamination rule).
