# Buyer Forces (ICP): the 17 questions

Exported verbatim from the Factor8 icps-form. One full pass per persona; one persona minimum, repeat for more. Naming note: Forces = ICP; "Feelings" is the older in-app alias for this same layer.

## Setup

1. **icp_name** (required): What is the name of your ideal customer? Example: "Stewart Startup, Marketing Maya, Corporate Carlos."

## Level 1: qualification

2. **demographic_requirements** (required): What roles and characteristics does the BUYER (the person) need to have to qualify? Role, location, involvement.
3. **firmographic_requirements** (required): What minimum criteria must the buyer's COMPANY meet to qualify for a sales conversation? Help: what is REQUIRED to do business with you. Example: "B2B SaaS selling to US, $1M-$5M revenue, solution LTV minimum $25,000."
4. **technographic_requirements** (required): What tools and technologies must customers use, or be willing to use, to buy from you? Example: "Has, or is willing to use, HubSpot."
5. **top_priority** (required): What is a top priority your ideal customer needs to solve NOW? Example: "Needs to see marketing ROI in 90 days."
6. **long_term_outcome** (required): What long-term outcome do they want? Example: "A predictable, scalable revenue machine that doesn't depend on the founder's network."
7. **trigger_events** (required): What events typically trigger them to start looking for a solution like yours? Example: "Missed revenue targets, burned marketing budget without results, board pressure."

## Level 2: psychology

8. **trusted_creators_and_communities** (required): What creators, influencers, or communities do they trust and follow?
9. **other_target_customer_details** (optional): Anything else important about this buyer.
10. **goals_or_dreams** (required): Their biggest goals or dreams.
11. **fears** (required): Their biggest fears. Example: "Wasting money on marketing that doesn't work, falling behind competitors, running out of runway."
12. **failures** (required): What failures or setbacks have they likely experienced? Example: "Trusted agencies that overpromised and underdelivered."
13. **frustrations** (required): What frustrates them the most, in their own words if possible.

## Level 3: the sharp edges (scrape rarely answers these; expect the human)

14. **enemies** (required): Who or what do they see as their enemy or biggest obstacle?
15. **suspicions** (required): What VALID suspicions or doubts do they have about solutions in your industry? Example: "Another agency making big promises; hidden costs; might need too much CEO time."
16. **false_beliefs** (required): What false beliefs do they hold that prevent them from succeeding? Example: "More traffic equals more revenue; referrals are enough to sustain growth."
17. **bottom_line_result** (required): The bottom-line result they want. Example: "Predictable, profitable growth that doesn't depend on luck or constant hustle."
