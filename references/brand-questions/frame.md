# Selling Frame: the 9 questions

Exported verbatim from the Factor8 frames-form.

## Level 1

1. **unique_value_proposition** (required): What makes your solution unique compared to competitors? Help template: "We help ____ (target customer) [verb] ____ (positive business outcome) in just ____ (achievable timeframe) using ____ (believable system), so they can ____ (transformation) without ____ (villain/roadblock)." Example: "Lean Labs helps funded tech startups with $2M-$10M install a profitable Growth Machine to drive compound growth every 90 days using Lean Growth Stacking."
2. **misconceptions** (required): What common misconceptions do people have about these types of solutions in your industry?
3. **villain_or_roadblock** (required): What are the major obstacles preventing your ideal customer from succeeding? Help: the barriers that block their goals without your solution.

## Level 2

4. **future_status** (required): After using your solution, what future status or position will your audience be in?
5. **how_it_works** (required): How it works: the mechanism, in plain steps.
6. **three_proven_steps_to_result** (required): The three key steps your company follows to reliably achieve results. Help: a methodical approach that can be understood and potentially replicated.

## Level 3

7. **believable_system** (required): What key methods or tools do you use to achieve results? Help: the named method or process that differentiates you. Example: "Lean Growth Stacking: win the warm market first."
8. **why_this_works** (required): Why do these steps work effectively? Justify each step's contribution.
