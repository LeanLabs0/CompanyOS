# Positioning prompts

Adapted from the Factor8 frames-form. These are optional prompts for positioning work. Use only questions relevant to the current task, and leave unsupported claims unknown.

## Level 1

1. **unique_value_proposition**: What makes your solution unique compared to competitors? Help template: "We help ____ (target customer) [verb] ____ (positive business outcome) in just ____ (achievable timeframe) using ____ (believable system), so they can ____ (transformation) without ____ (villain/roadblock)." Example: "Lean Labs helps funded tech startups with $2M-$10M install a profitable Growth Machine to drive compound growth every 90 days using Lean Growth Stacking."
2. **misconceptions**: What common misconceptions do people have about these types of solutions in your industry?
3. **villain_or_roadblock**: What are the major obstacles preventing your ideal customer from succeeding? Help: the barriers that block their goals without your solution.

## Level 2

4. **future_status**: After using your solution, what future status or position will your audience be in?
5. **how_it_works**: How it works: the mechanism, in plain steps.
6. **three_proven_steps_to_result**: The key steps your company follows to achieve results; use the number of steps the actual process needs. Help: a methodical approach that can be understood and potentially replicated.

## Level 3

7. **believable_system**: What key methods or tools do you use to achieve results? Help: the named method or process that differentiates you. Example: "Lean Growth Stacking: win the warm market first."
8. **why_this_works**: Why do these steps work effectively? Justify each step's contribution.
