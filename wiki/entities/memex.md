# Memex

Vannevar Bush's 1945 concept of a personal, curated knowledge store with associative trails between documents (from "As We May Think").

## Facts

- Private and actively curated; the connections between documents were as valuable as the documents themselves - per [LLM Wiki idea file](../sources/llm-wiki-idea.md).
- Closer in spirit to the LLM Wiki pattern than to what the web became - per [LLM Wiki idea file](../sources/llm-wiki-idea.md).
- The unsolved part of Bush's vision was who does the maintenance; the [LLM Wiki pattern](../concepts/llm-wiki-pattern.md) answers that with the LLM - per [LLM Wiki idea file](../sources/llm-wiki-idea.md).
